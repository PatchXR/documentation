bell_filter
===========

.. _bell_filter:

**Description**

Boosts or cuts a specific frequency band

Boosts or cuts a specific frequency band

**Inputs, output and other parts**

*input* (stream input) The input signal to filter

*Frequency* (stream input) The frequency to boost or cut

*Gain* (stream input) How much to boost or cut the selected frequency.

*Bandwidth* (stream input) How much frequencies around the center will be boosted or cut.

*Output* (stream output) The filtered output signal
