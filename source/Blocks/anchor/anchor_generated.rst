anchor
======

.. _anchor:

**Description**

Your key to teleportation! Simply trigger this block to instantly transport yourself to its location and rotation. Send a positive number to gradually transport yourself over time (in seconds)



**Inputs, output and other parts**

*Direction* (pointer) After teleporting, the mask of the player will be facing in this direction (notice long tube)

*Teleport* (jolt input) send 0 to teleport instantly or positive numbers (in seconds)
